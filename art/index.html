Between-week overlaps

The week with more total hours controls the overlap, using its touching bubble’s sport color and blend mode. Opacity also applies here.

Composition

Spring strength pulls bubbles toward their own week center. Collisions also act between neighboring weeks, letting clusters turn and reshape into open space. Size dominance keeps larger bubbles closer in.

Paint

Every daily circle becomes a wash of pigment. Its area still encodes hours: outlines wobble, but each shape keeps the circle’s area.

Session data

Data drive steers each blob by its session. Effort sets the aggression: darker, more numerous, more jagged discoloration on hard days. It is also the chance that this table picks the texture rather than the Texture mix below. At 1 the session decides; at 0 everything is left to chance.

Rows are checked top to bottom; the first match wins. Effort and variability are percentiles within each sport. Days without effort data use the Texture mix unless climbing, duration, or variability places them.
